Quiet Frontiers

A personal automation that writes, renders and schedules short documentary videos for its owner’s own YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What this application does

This is a single-operator publishing tool. Once a day it selects a topic, writes a narration script, synthesises a voiceover, generates background footage, assembles a widescreen video and a vertical short, and uploads both to one YouTube channel — the one owned by the person who installed it. Each upload is scheduled to become public at a fixed time of day.

It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no public interface. Nobody other than the operator authorises it, and it never reads or writes anyone else’s channel.

Why it asks for YouTube access

The application uses YouTube API Services to place finished videos on the operator’s own channel. It requests exactly two permissions and nothing else:

PermissionWhy it is needed
youtube.upload To upload the rendered video files to the operator’s channel.
youtube.force-ssl To set the title, description, tags, custom thumbnail and scheduled publication time on those same uploads.

The application does not read subscriber data, comments, analytics, or any other channel information, and it does not modify videos it did not upload itself.

How access is granted and revoked

The operator signs in once through Google’s standard consent screen. The resulting credential is stored as an encrypted secret in the operator’s own automation environment and is used only to upload to their channel. Access can be withdrawn at any moment from Google Account permissions, which immediately stops all further uploads.

This application’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Content

Videos are produced with automated writing, speech synthesis and video generation tools. The operator is responsible for everything published on the channel and reviews the system’s output. Corrections and content questions should be sent to the contact address in the Privacy Policy.

By using YouTube API Services, this application is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
